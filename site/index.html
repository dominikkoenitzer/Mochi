Mochi

Mochi arranges every open window side by side so nothing overlaps, and you move between them from the keyboard.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. No admin rights needed.

The moment it starts, every window becomes a tile. Pause turns tiling off and on, and Alt+Shift+E stops Mochi and puts every window back.

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mochi runs on Windows. Open this page on your PC to download it.

Layouts

Seven layouts, one key away

Mochi tiles in BSP, columns, rows, vertical stack, horizontal stack, ultrawide and grid. Alt+Shift+V steps through them.

Workspaces

Nine workspaces on every monitor

Alt+1 to Alt+9 switch workspace, and Alt+Shift with a number takes the focused window along.

Each workspace keeps its own layout, so one can be in columns while another stays in BSP.

Float

Float a window above the tiles

Alt+Shift+Space takes the focused window out of the layout and leaves it at its own size and place, above the others. Press it again to tile it.

Resize

Resize tiles from the keyboard

Alt+Shift+S enters resize mode: H and L make the window narrower and wider, J and K taller and shorter, and Esc leaves. The tiles next to it make room.

Scripting

Every answer as JSON

mochic drives Mochi from a terminal or a script, and every command that reports something takes --json. mochic why says why a window is or is not tiled.

The hotkey file is plain text and is reloaded the moment you save it.

AI assistant

Arrange windows by asking

mochic mcp lets an AI assistant see your monitors, workspaces and windows and arrange them: focus a window, move it, switch workspaces, set the layout.

No tool can stop Mochi, close a window or write a file, and nothing leaves your PC. Download the skill to teach your assistant the rest.

Add Mochi to your MCP client (replace you with your Windows user name)

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "mochi": {
      "command": "C:\\Users\\you\\AppData\\Local\\Programs\\Mochi\\bin\\mochic.exe",
      "args": ["mcp"]
    }
  }
}

Privacy

Nothing leaves your PC

Mochi makes no network connection and needs no account. The assistant connection uses the same local pipe as mochic.

Your data

Two plain text files

Settings live in %USERPROFILE%\mochi.json, hotkeys in %USERPROFILE%\.config\mochi\hotkeys. A log a day goes to %LOCALAPPDATA%\mochi, and the last 14 are kept.

Keyboard

From the default hotkey file. Every key can be changed in it.

  • Focus left, down, up, rightAlt+H/J/K/L
  • Move the windowAlt+Shift+H/J/K/L
  • Switch workspaceAlt+1–9
  • Resize modeAlt+Shift+S
  • Float, or tile againAlt+Shift+Space
  • Next layoutAlt+Shift+V
  • Tiling off and onPause
  • Stop Mochi, every window backAlt+Shift+E

Install, update and remove

01

Get started

  1. Download Mochi-Setup.exe and open it.
  2. Click Install. Mochi starts and tiles every window.
  3. Hold Alt and press H, J, K or L to move between windows.
02

Updates

Download Mochi-Setup.exe again and open it. It stops the running Mochi first and leaves your settings and hotkeys alone.

03

Removing it

Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Mochi, Uninstall. Every window goes back where it was. Your settings and hotkeys stay until you delete them.

Questions

Is Mochi free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Why does Windows say it protected my PC?

Mochi is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows warns about it the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. Setup needs no admin rights and installs only for you.

How do I get my normal desktop back?

Pause, or Alt+F12 on a keyboard without a Pause key, turns tiling off and leaves every window where it is. Alt+Shift+E stops Mochi and puts every window back.

A window is not being tiled. Why?

Run mochic why with that window in front. It says what Mochi makes of the window and what you can do about it. A program running as administrator cannot be moved, because Mochi runs without admin rights.

Where does Mochi keep my data?

Settings in %USERPROFILE%\mochi.json, hotkeys in %USERPROFILE%\.config\mochi\hotkeys, and the log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\mochi. Nothing leaves your PC.
