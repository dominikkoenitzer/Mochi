Mochi

Mochi arranges every open window side by side so nothing overlaps, and you move between them from the keyboard.

Download for Windows

For 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. It needs no admin rights.

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mochi runs on Windows. Open this page on your Windows PC to download it.

Get going

  1. Open Mochi-Setup.exe from your downloads and click Install.
  2. Every window on your screen moves into a tile of its own at once. Mochi starts again by itself each time you sign in.
  3. Hold Alt and press H, J, K or L to jump to the window on the left, below, above or to the right.

Getting your windows back

  • Pause turns tiling off and on. Off leaves every window where it is. On a keyboard without a Pause key, press Alt+F12.
  • Alt+Shift+E stops Mochi and puts every window back. To start it again, open Mochi from the Start menu.
  • To remove Mochi, open Settings, then Apps, find Mochi in the list and uninstall it.

More keys

  • Alt+Shift with H, J, K or L moves the window that way.
  • Alt+1 to Alt+9 switch between nine workspaces, each with its own windows. Add Shift to take the window along.
  • Alt+Shift+Space lets a window float above the others, or puts it back in its tile.
  • Alt+Shift+S lets you resize the window with H, J, K and L until you press Esc.
  • Alt+Shift+Q closes the window.
